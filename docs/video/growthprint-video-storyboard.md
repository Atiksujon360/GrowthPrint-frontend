# GrowthPrint explainer: storyboard

The film is 1920×1080 at 30 fps and runs 60 s, with a draft narration track (see the script). Every frame is drawn by `scripts/marketing-media/explainer.html` and is reproducible. Nothing shown is a client, a result or a statistic. Interface elements are illustrative, carry no figures, and are presented as illustrations.

| # | Time | Picture | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–7 s | Six disconnected tool windows ("Old website", "Spreadsheet", "Email inbox", "Invoices", "Customer list", "Copy and paste") drift apart with broken red dashed links. At 5 s violet light trails connect them. | Your business deserves better technology. |
| 2 | 7–14 s | The official GrowthPrint mark and wordmark, with the metallic sculpture turning slowly on the right. | Meet GrowthPrint. / Your AI-native technology partner. (Source updated 16 Nov 2026; the current render still shows "Your complete technology partner" until the next render.) |
